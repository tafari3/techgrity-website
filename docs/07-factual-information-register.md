# Techgrity Systems Website Factual Information Register

**Status:** ACTIVE CONTROL REGISTER  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Owner:** Techgrity Systems website programme

## 1. Purpose

This register prevents provisional, unknown or unsupported information from becoming public fact. Every corporate, contact, legal, partner, certification, project and quantitative claim must have an explicit status and evidence source before publication.

The register is authoritative for website claim control. Absence of a fact from this register does not authorise publication.

## 2. Status model

| Status | Meaning | Publication rule |
|---|---|---|
| `CONFIRMED` | Directly approved and operationally verified | May be published in approved wording |
| `PROVISIONAL` | Present in approved design or planning but still requires final operational or legal verification | May appear only in non-production prototypes; not final publication |
| `BLOCKED` | Required for launch or a specific feature but evidence is missing | Must be resolved or omitted before launch |
| `OMIT` | Not required for launch or deliberately excluded | Do not display |
| `FUTURE` | Valid future content category with no launch evidence | Keep unpublished until promoted through review |
| `SUPERSEDED` | Replaced by a newer approved value | Remove or redirect as appropriate |

## 3. Evidence standard

Acceptable evidence may include:

- official company registration documents;
- written approval from an authorised Techgrity representative;
- direct operational verification of telephone, email, address or form routing;
- executed partner agreement or public partner directory entry;
- certification document and issuing-body verification;
- client publication approval;
- project completion and acceptance evidence;
- approved policy or legal document;
- production-system verification tied to an exact release.

Screenshots, mock-ups, draft documents and prior website copy are not sufficient by themselves for high-risk claims.

## 13. Image and asset provenance

Every production asset requires a separate asset register entry with:

- filename;
- source or generated provenance;
- licence or ownership;
- subject and communication purpose;

- desktop and mobile crops;

## 15. Pre-publication fact gate

Before any page is approved for production, reviewers must answer:

1. Which statements on this page are factual claims?
2. Is each claim present in this register?
3. Is the status `CONFIRMED`?
4. Does the public wording match the evidence scope?
5. Does any image imply a client, team, project, location or partnership?
6. Are numbers current and traceable?
7. Are capability statements prospective where public delivery evidence is absent?
8. Are legal and privacy statements aligned with actual implementation?

Any `PROVISIONAL` or `BLOCKED` fact must be resolved, removed or replaced with approved neutral wording.
