## Technology Supply & Integration

### Route

`/capabilities/technology-supply`

### Audience and user intent

Procurement teams, institutional decision-makers, technical evaluators and partners need to know whether Techgrity can translate a requirement into a correctly specified, sourced, installed, integrated and commissioned solution.

### Business objective

Present technology supply as a governed project-delivery capability connecting Digital Systems & AI with Infrastructure & Engineering, not as a third unrelated division or an online product catalogue.

### SEO intent

Technology equipment sourcing, supply and installation, systems integration, commissioning, OEM-backed solutions and managed support.

### Browser title

`Technology Supply & Integration | Techgrity Systems`

### H1

> From technical requirement to installed and commissioned solution

### Hero support

> Techgrity coordinates specifications, sourcing, logistics, installation, configuration, integration, testing and operational handover across digital and infrastructure projects.

Primary CTA: `Discuss a Supply or Integration Project`  
Secondary CTA: `How We Deliver`

### Ordered section structure

#### 1. Positioning

Explain that procurement is one stage in a controlled delivery process. The correct outcome depends on:

- understanding the operating requirement;
- selecting compatible technology;
- defining responsibilities;
- preparing the environment;
- installing and configuring correctly;
- integrating with existing systems;
- testing against acceptance criteria;
- documenting and supporting the result.

#### 2. Requirements assessment

Cover:

- intended users and services;
- current environment;
- capacity and performance;
- compatibility;
- availability and resilience;
- security;
- facilities and power;
- licensing and warranty;
- support capability;
- future expansion;
- budget and timescale.

The page must make clear that final selection depends on validated requirements.

#### 3. Technical specifications and bills of materials

H2:

> Specifications before purchasing decisions

Content must cover:

- technical requirements;
- mandatory and optional features;
- quantities;
- compatibility;
- accessories and supporting infrastructure;
- licensing;
- installation services;
- testing requirements;
- spares or lifecycle considerations;
- acceptance criteria.

Avoid public model-specific recommendations that may become stale unless part of a maintained resource.

#### 4. OEM-backed and partner-supported delivery

Use only responsible, evidence-bound wording:

> Where a project requires specialist products, authorised channels or certified services, Techgrity coordinates appropriate suppliers and qualified delivery partners under clearly defined responsibilities.

Rules:

- do not show partner or OEM logos without documented permission;
- do not use “authorised”, “certified”, “gold partner” or similar status unless proven;
- do not imply Techgrity itself provides every specialist service;
- identify warranty and support responsibility in project documentation.

#### 5. Equipment sourcing

Cover:

- approved sources;
- authenticity and traceability where applicable;
- compatibility checks;
- warranty terms;
- licensing;
- lead times;
- substitutions and change approval;
- commercial and technical evaluation.

Do not promise inventory or delivery times generically.

#### 6. Logistics and delivery coordination

Cover:

- delivery scheduling;
- site readiness;
- transport and handling requirements;
- receiving and inspection;
- asset identification;
- damage or discrepancy handling;
- controlled storage where required.

#### 7. Installation and configuration

Explain that installation includes the environment and configuration required for equipment to operate correctly, such as:

- mounting or placement;
- power and grounding interfaces;
- cabling and connectivity;
- software or firmware configuration;
- security settings;
- identity and network integration;
- baseline records.

#### 8. Systems integration

Show how supplied equipment or platforms may need to integrate with:

- networks;
- servers and storage;
- applications;
- identity and access;
- monitoring;
- data platforms;
- physical security;
- power and facility systems.

Link to `/capabilities/digital-systems/integration`.

#### 9. Testing and commissioning

Potential activities:

- receiving inspection;
- installation checks;
- functional testing;
- configuration verification;
- integration testing;
- performance or capacity checks where specified;
- failure-path testing where relevant;
- defect correction;
- acceptance evidence.

Commissioning must be framed as verification against agreed requirements, not merely switching equipment on.

#### 10. Documentation and asset handover

Potential deliverables:

- approved specification and bill of materials;
- supplier and warranty records;
- serial or asset schedules;
- configuration records;
- diagrams;
- test and commissioning evidence;
- licences or entitlement information;
- operating procedures;
- training records;
- support and escalation information.

#### 11. Training and operational handover

Cover:

- administrator or operator orientation;
- routine checks;
- permitted changes;
- fault and escalation paths;
- warranty process;
- support responsibilities;
- knowledge transfer.

Do not promise formal certification training unless provided by an authorised party.

#### 12. Maintenance and support arrangements

Possible arrangements:

- preventive maintenance;
- configuration support;
- warranty coordination;
- fault isolation;
- replacement planning;
- monitoring where agreed;
- controlled upgrades;
- lifecycle reviews.

No response time or service level may be stated without an approved support model.

#### 13. Technology categories

The page may show broad, non-catalogue categories:

**Digital platforms**

- application and integration platforms;
- identity and access technology;
- data and analytics platforms;
- endpoint and user technology where in scope.

**Networks and connectivity**

- switching and routing;
- wireless infrastructure;
- fibre and structured cabling;
- microwave and communications equipment.

**Data-centre and computing infrastructure**

- servers;
- storage;
- racks and accessories;
- virtualisation and backup platforms;
- monitoring.

**Power and facility systems**

- UPS and batteries;
- backup and hybrid power components;
- environmental monitoring;
- CCTV and access-control systems;
- technical-room and shelter equipment.

Rules:

- no prices;
- no shopping cart;
- no “in stock” claims;
- no fixed model lists unless actively maintained;
- no vendor logos without approval.

#### 14. Delivery model

Show the relevant lifecycle:

1. Assess
2. Specify
3. Source
4. Prepare
5. Install
6. Integrate
7. Commission
8. Handover
9. Support

Link to `/how-we-deliver` for complete governance.

#### 15. Representative delivery patterns

Clearly label these as examples, not completed client projects:

- campus network equipment, fibre, racks, power protection and commissioning;
- server and storage platform with virtualisation, backup and network integration;
- telecom site equipment with shelter, power, backhaul, security and monitoring;
- access-control and CCTV environment integrated with identities, networks and operational procedures;
- software platform requiring cloud or on-premise infrastructure, integrations, deployment and support.

### Imagery and diagrams

Required visual direction:

- structured equipment and system-context photography;
- requirement-to-handover process graphic;
- bill-of-materials or integration diagram presented abstractly;
- installation and commissioning imagery with credible safety and technical practice.

Avoid:

- product catalogue grids;
- isolated consumer electronics;
- price labels;
- warehouses implying unverified stock levels;
- vendor-logo walls;
- generic handshakes as the primary visual.

### Factual dependencies

- confirmed sourcing territories and logistics capability;
- approved supplier and partner relationships;
- warranty responsibility model;
- any import, customs or tax statements;
- supported technology platforms;
- support and maintenance terms;
- public procurement and tender response wording.

### Acceptance criteria

- the page is clearly a delivery capability, not a shop;
- Technology Supply & Integration visibly connects both specialist divisions;
- requirement assessment, specification, installation, integration and commissioning are all explicit;
- no unverified OEM or authorised-partner claim appears;
- no product price, stock or guaranteed lead time appears;
- representative patterns cannot be misread as completed projects;
- documentation, warranty and support responsibility are addressed;
- all calls to action route correctly.
