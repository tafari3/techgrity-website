# Legal and System Pages — Content Specification

**Status:** BASELINE DRAFT  
**Public pages covered:** 3  
**Supporting system experiences covered:** 5

## Legal-content rule

Legal pages must describe the website's actual data processing, technologies, ownership and operational practices. They must not be copied from unrelated websites or populated with assumptions. Final legal wording should be reviewed against Zimbabwean law and any other applicable jurisdictional obligations before publication.

---

## 1. Privacy Policy

### Route

`/privacy`

### Audience and user intent

Website visitors, enquiry submitters and document requesters want to understand what personal information is collected, why it is used, where it is sent, how it is protected and how to raise a privacy request.

### Business objective

Provide an accurate, understandable privacy notice that matches the live website and enquiry-handling process.

### SEO and indexing intent

Indexable legal page. No marketing keyword objective.

### Browser title

`Privacy Policy | Techgrity Systems`

### H1

> Privacy Policy

### Introductory statement direction

Identify the official legal entity operating the website, the website domain, the policy effective date and the purpose of the notice.

### Required section order

#### 1. Who operates this website

Must include:

- verified legal company name;
- jurisdiction of registration;
- approved public address;
- privacy contact email;
- role of Techgrity as controller or equivalent under applicable law where appropriate.

#### 2. Information collected directly

Describe only fields actually used, such as:

- contact information;
- organisation and role;
- project or enquiry details;
- document request information;
- uploaded files if enabled;
- correspondence and follow-up information.

#### 3. Information collected automatically

Based on the final implementation, potentially include:

- IP address;
- device and browser information;
- server logs;
- security events;
- cookie and analytics data;
- page and interaction information.

No category may appear unless it is actually processed.

#### 4. Purposes of processing

Potential purposes:

- respond to enquiries;
- qualify and route project opportunities;
- provide requested documents;
- protect the website and forms;
- maintain and improve website performance;
- comply with legal and recordkeeping obligations;
- measure website use where consent or another lawful basis applies.

#### 5. Lawful or permitted grounds

The final legal review must define the applicable grounds, which may include consent, steps requested before entering a contract, legitimate business interests and legal obligations.

#### 6. Recipients and service providers

Identify categories and named providers where required, including:

- hosting provider;
- email or transactional message provider;
- form backend;
- analytics provider;
- spam protection;
- file storage;
- professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

#### 7. International transfers

Explain if any service provider processes information outside Zimbabwe or another relevant jurisdiction and what protections apply.

#### 8. Retention

Define actual retention periods or decision criteria for:

- general enquiries;
- project enquiries;
- tender documents;
- uploaded files;
- server and security logs;
- analytics data.

Avoid indefinite or vague retention unless justified.

#### 9. Security

Describe proportionate organisational and technical safeguards without disclosing sensitive defensive details or claiming absolute security.

#### 10. Rights and requests

Explain applicable rights and how to submit a request. Final wording must be jurisdictionally reviewed.

#### 11. Cookies and analytics

Summarise and link to `/cookies` and the cookie-preference interface.

#### 12. Third-party links

Clarify responsibility boundaries for external websites.

#### 13. Children

State intended audience and handling, based on actual service and legal advice. Do not use boilerplate that conflicts with future education-sector enquiries.

#### 14. Changes to this policy

State how the effective date and material updates will be shown.

#### 15. Contact

Provide the approved privacy contact.

### CTAs

Primary: `Contact Us About Privacy`  
Secondary: `Manage Cookie Preferences`

### Factual dependencies

- official legal entity;
- physical address;
- privacy mailbox;
- form fields;
- hosting, email, analytics, spam and storage providers;
- data locations;
- retention schedule;
- applicable legal review.

### Acceptance criteria

- page matches actual website processing;
- all named providers and fields are correct;
- retention is defined;
- cookie statements match the live consent system;
- no claim of absolute security;
- version and effective date are visible.

## 2. Terms of Use

### Route

`/terms`

### Audience and user intent

Visitors want to understand website ownership, permitted use, document use, enquiry limitations and responsibility boundaries.

### Business objective

Set reasonable conditions for use of the public website and downloadable materials without turning the page into an unreadable or misleading contract.

### SEO and indexing intent

Indexable legal page. No marketing keyword objective.

### Browser title

`Terms of Use | Techgrity Systems`

### H1

> Website Terms of Use

### Required section order

#### 1. Operator and acceptance

Identify the verified legal entity and explain that use of the website is subject to the terms.

#### 2. Informational purpose

Explain that website content provides general corporate and capability information and does not, by itself, create a contract, technical design, professional advice, quotation or binding commitment.

#### 3. Project enquiries

Clarify:

- submitting a form does not guarantee acceptance;
- any scope, proposal, price, timeline or contract is handled separately;
- users must provide accurate and lawful information;
- confidential, secret or unnecessary sensitive information should not be submitted through general forms.

#### 4. Capability documents

Define permitted business and evaluation use of downloadable or requested documents, and restrictions on alteration, misleading attribution or unauthorised redistribution where appropriate.

#### 5. Intellectual property

Cover ownership or licensed use of:

- logo and brand;
- written content;
- diagrams and designs;
- photographs and media;
- downloadable documents;
- website code where relevant.

#### 6. Acceptable use

Prohibit activities such as:

- unlawful use;
- interference with the website;
- unauthorised access attempts;
- malicious uploads;
- automated extraction that materially burdens the service;
- impersonation;
- infringement of rights.

#### 7. External links and third parties

Explain that links do not automatically constitute endorsement and that third-party services have their own terms.

#### 8. Accuracy and changes

Explain that Techgrity aims for accuracy but may update capabilities, documents and website content. This must not be used to excuse false claims or broken documents.

#### 9. Availability

Avoid guaranteeing uninterrupted website availability. State that maintenance or technical issues may occur.

#### 10. Responsibility limitations

Final wording requires legal review and must remain reasonable under applicable law. It must not attempt to exclude non-excludable responsibilities.

#### 11. Privacy

Link to `/privacy` and `/cookies`.

#### 12. Governing law and disputes

Use verified, legally reviewed jurisdiction and dispute wording.

#### 13. Contact

Provide the approved legal or business contact.

### CTAs

Primary: `Contact Techgrity`  
Secondary: `Read the Privacy Policy`

### Factual dependencies

- legal entity and jurisdiction;
- intellectual-property ownership;
- document distribution rules;
- approved governing-law wording;
- legal contact.

### Acceptance criteria

- wording matches the website's actual functions;
- form submission limitations are clear;
- documents and IP are addressed;
- no unlawful or overbroad disclaimer is published without review;
- effective date is visible.

## 3. Cookie Policy

### Route

`/cookies`

### Audience and user intent

Visitors want to know which cookies or similar technologies are used and how to control optional processing.

### Business objective

Provide a transparent, accurate explanation that matches the live cookie inventory and consent mechanism.

### SEO and indexing intent

Indexable legal page. No marketing keyword objective.

### Browser title

`Cookie Policy | Techgrity Systems`

### H1

> Cookie Policy

### Required section order

#### 1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Use plain language and include local storage or similar browser storage if used.

#### 2. Technologies used by this website

Create an implementation-derived inventory with categories such as:

**Strictly necessary**

Examples may include security, load balancing, form state or consent preference storage.

**Preferences**

Only if the website remembers optional user choices.

**Analytics**

Only if an approved analytics tool is implemented.

**Marketing**

Launch default should be none unless explicitly approved.

#### 3. Cookie table

For each technology include:

- name;
- provider;
- purpose;
- category;
- duration;
- first-party or third-party status.

Do not publish placeholder entries.

#### 4. Consent and control

Explain:

- which technologies run without optional consent and why;
- how optional categories are accepted or rejected;
- how visitors can reopen preferences;
- effect of withdrawing consent;
- browser-level controls.

#### 5. Third-party technologies

Identify providers and link to their relevant policies only when used.

#### 6. Policy updates

State how changes and effective dates are shown.

#### 7. Contact

Provide the privacy contact.

### CTAs

Primary: `Manage Cookie Preferences`  
Secondary: `Read the Privacy Policy`

### Factual dependencies

- final cookie and storage inventory;
- analytics decision;
- spam and security tool behaviour;
- consent platform;
- provider documentation;
- privacy contact.

### Acceptance criteria

- cookie table is generated from or reconciled with the live implementation;
- no optional analytics or marketing technology runs before the required choice;
- preference control is always accessible;
- policy, banner and application behaviour agree.

# Supporting System Experiences

## 4. 404 — Page Not Found

### Route

`/404` and server fallback for unknown public routes.

### User objective

Recover from a missing, moved or mistyped route without losing confidence or becoming trapped.

### H1

> We could not find that page

### Support copy

> The address may be incorrect, or the page may have moved. Use the links below to continue.

### Required actions

Primary: `Return to Home`  
Secondary: `Explore Capabilities`

Additional links:

- Industries;
- Resources;
- Contact;
- Discuss a Project.

### Optional function

A site search should appear only if a real, maintained search capability is implemented.

### Visual direction

Use a restrained technical route or connection motif. Do not use a childish broken-robot illustration.

### Acceptance criteria

- returns HTTP 404 for unknown routes;
- navigation and footer remain usable;
- no broken asset or console error;
- links route correctly;
- page is excluded from XML sitemap and normal indexing.

## 5. Project Enquiry Received

### Route

`/project-enquiry-received`

### Entry condition

Displayed only after the project enquiry has been successfully accepted by the server-side form workflow. Direct access should not create a false submission record.

### H1

> Your project enquiry has been received

### Support copy

> Thank you for sharing the requirement. The information will be reviewed and routed to the appropriate Techgrity capability or business contact.

Do not promise a response time unless approved.

### Required content

- confirmation that submission succeeded;
- reference number only if the backend generates a real one;
- reminder not to resend unless the user receives an error or needs to add information;
- business contact details;
- privacy link.

### Actions

Primary: `Return to Home`  
Secondary: `Explore How We Deliver`

### Acceptance criteria

- page is shown only after confirmed acceptance;
- no sensitive form content is repeated in the URL or page source;
- refresh does not resubmit the form;
- analytics conversion event fires only after real success;
- page should normally be excluded from search indexing.

## 7. Form Submission Error

### Route

`/form-error` or inline error state, depending on final implementation.

### User objective

Understand that the submission was not accepted, preserve entered information where safe and know how to recover.

### H1

> We could not submit your information

### Support copy

> Your enquiry has not been confirmed. Review the highlighted fields or try again. You can also contact Techgrity directly using the details below.

### Required behaviour

- preserve non-sensitive entered values where technically safe;
- explain whether validation, file, network or server failure occurred without exposing internal details;
- provide a retry action;
- provide direct email and telephone alternatives;
- provide an error reference only if generated by the backend;
- never falsely state that a submission succeeded.

### Actions

Primary: `Return to the Form`  
Secondary: `Email Techgrity`

### Acceptance criteria

- no silent failure;
- user can recover without re-entering everything where possible;
- errors are accessible and specific;
- server details, stack traces and credentials are not exposed;
- duplicate submissions are controlled.

## 8. Cookie Preference Interface

### Route or interface

Persistent control accessible from the footer and Cookie Policy. It may be a dialog or dedicated panel rather than an indexable page.

### Title

> Cookie preferences

### Required categories

- Strictly necessary — always active, with explanation;
- Preferences — only if used;
- Analytics — only if used;
- Marketing — launch default should be absent unless approved.

### Required controls

- Accept selected;
- Reject optional;
- Accept all, only if optional categories exist;
- category-level toggles;
- link to Cookie Policy;
- saved-state confirmation.

### Behaviour rules

- optional technologies must wait for applicable consent;
- withdrawal must be as easy as acceptance;
- preference storage must be documented;
- interface must be keyboard accessible;
- focus must be managed correctly;
- controls must not use dark patterns;
- rejecting optional cookies must not block ordinary website use.

### Acceptance criteria

- technical behaviour matches the policy;
- consent is recorded appropriately;
- choice persists for the approved period;
- users can reopen and change the choice;
- no optional script runs before the required consent state.
