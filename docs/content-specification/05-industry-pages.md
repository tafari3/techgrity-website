# Industry Pages — Content Specification

**Status:** BASELINE DRAFT  
**Pages covered:** 6

## Shared industry-page rule

Every industry page must explain sector relevance through operating conditions, responsibilities and representative solution patterns. It must not present those patterns as completed Techgrity case studies unless approved evidence exists.

Each page uses this common sequence:

1. sector-specific hero;
2. operating environment;
3. common challenges;
4. digital-system requirements;
5. infrastructure requirements;
6. integrated solution map;
7. relevant capabilities;
8. representative project types;
9. delivery and governance considerations;
10. related sectors;
11. final CTA.
