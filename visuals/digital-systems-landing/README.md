**Route:** `/capabilities/digital-systems`

**Wave:** 1 — Corporate structure  
**Status:** `REVIEW`

## Purpose

This master visual establishes Techgrity as a serious Digital Systems and AI delivery organisation rather than a basic website-development agency. It routes visitors into the five digital capability families while explaining how users, workflows, applications, data, integration, controls and infrastructure form one operational system.

## Governing content

- H1: `Digital systems built around how organisations actually operate`;
- primary CTA: `Discuss a Digital Project`;
- secondary CTA: `Explore Digital Capabilities`;
- operating-requirement positioning;
- six representative organisational challenges;
- five digital capability routes;
- representative system types;
- wider operating-environment architecture;
- practical AI with human accountability;
- security, controls and evidence;
- Discover-to-Support digital lifecycle;
- connected Infrastructure capability;
- final digital-project CTA.

## Visual direction

- lighter white and pale-neutral surfaces dominate;
- teal carries digital-system emphasis and active paths;
- the hero combines credible people-at-work photography with restrained conceptual interface panels;
- no robot, glowing-brain or science-fiction imagery appears;
- interface previews are clearly conceptual and do not claim an existing public product;
- architecture, AI and accountability are shown through labelled diagrams with visible text equivalents;
- dark navy sections are reserved for architecture and control authority;
- the visual remains recognisably part of the approved homepage system.

## Responsive decisions

- architecture uses a labelled four-layer diagram;
- AI accountability uses a human-centred radial diagram;
- control and lifecycle sections use structured horizontal layouts.

### Mobile

- utility bar is omitted, matching the approved homepage mobile shell;
- hero becomes copy-first and all CTAs are full width;
- design principles become a vertical list;
- diagrams, capability cards and system lists stack;
- architecture layers convert to two-column labelled grids;
- lifecycle reduces to a two-column sequence;
- no horizontal overflow is permitted.
