## Purpose

This master visual establishes Infrastructure & Engineering as a major specialist division with distinct capability categories, integrated project delivery, testing, documentation and responsible scope boundaries.

## Governing content

- H1: `Critical infrastructure designed for reliable operation`;
- primary CTA: `Discuss an Infrastructure Project`;
- secondary CTA: `Explore Infrastructure Capabilities`;
- infrastructure positioning around the complete operating environment;
- six capability routes;
- assessment and design model;
- supply, installation and commissioning lifecycle;
- Digital Systems and Infrastructure integration;
- quality, safety and project controls;
- representative industry contexts;
- support and lifecycle planning;
- final infrastructure-project CTA.

## Visual direction

- strong navy hero and technical photography establish engineering authority;
- data-centre, telecom, network and power imagery are combined without turning the page into a construction collage;
- capability cards use consistent photographic crops and specification-led content;
- assessment, commissioning and integrated-operation diagrams use precise labels and restrained technical geometry;
- white and ivory sections preserve readability and prevent excessive visual heaviness;
- no unsupported standard, licence, certification, OEM, contractor or project claim appears;
- Civil & Technical Infrastructure is framed as technical enabling works and qualified specialist coordination, not unrestricted general construction.
