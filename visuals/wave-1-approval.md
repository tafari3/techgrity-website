# Wave 1 Master-Visual Approval

**Decision:** APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORITY  
**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Templates:** Capabilities overview, Digital Systems landing, Infrastructure landing

## Production provenance

The three master templates were authored as real HTML, CSS and JavaScript under `visuals/prototypes/`, rendered in Chromium through Playwright and reviewed from desktop, tablet, mobile and full-page captures. They were not designed as generated static page images.

## Approved templates

### Capabilities overview

Approved because it:

- converts the broad portfolio into one coherent three-part operating model;
- gives Digital Systems and Infrastructure equal weight;
- presents Technology Supply & Integration as the connecting delivery capability;
- contains all required capability routes and representative solution patterns;
- avoids an unrelated icon wall, product catalogue or fabricated case-study treatment;
- remains coherent across desktop, tablet and mobile.

### Digital Systems landing

Approved because it:

- follows the lighter, human and workflow-led divisional expression;
- presents five distinct capability routes;
- explains systems, architecture, data, security, delivery and support;
- avoids AI robots, speculative dashboards and small web-agency positioning;
- remains coherent across desktop, tablet and mobile.

### Infrastructure landing

Approved because it:

- uses stronger navy, technical photography and engineering structure;
- presents all six infrastructure capability routes;
- explains connected layers, delivery method, quality controls, commissioning and support;
- avoids equipment-catalogue and unrestricted construction positioning;
- remains coherent across desktop, tablet and mobile.

These three templates may now govern their page families. Implementation may improve semantics, route wiring and performance, but material changes to composition, hierarchy or visual direction require a superseding reviewed reference and approval record.
