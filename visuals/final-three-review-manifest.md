# Final Three Master-Visual Review Manifest

**Date:** 29 July 2026  
**Status:** READY FOR USER REVIEW  
**Templates:** Company; Resources; Contact & Enquiry Forms

## Production method

All three templates were authored as real repository-backed HTML and CSS prototypes under `visuals/prototypes/final-three/` and rendered in Chromium through Playwright. No generated static webpage mockup was used as design authority.

## Hero decisions

### Company

One corporate operating model showing:

- Digital Systems & AI;
- Infrastructure & Engineering;
- Technology Supply & Project Delivery;
- governance, evidence, handover and support;
- the route from organisation requirement to integrated operational outcome.

No fake office, boardroom or team portrait is used.

### Resources

One document-publication and access framework showing:

- approval;
- registration and version metadata;
- controlled publication;
- maintenance and withdrawal of superseded files.

No document is presented as publicly downloadable before it exists and is approved.

### Contact & Enquiry Forms

One enquiry-routing framework showing three distinct routes:

- general enquiry;
- structured project enquiry;
- capability-document request.

The design separates the forms instead of overloading one contact form and makes no unsupported response-time, address or business-hours promise.

## Internal QA

All three templates passed:

- exactly one H1;
- natural full-page document height;
- 1672 px viewport with no horizontal overflow;
- approved logo loaded at intrinsic width;
- browser console with no errors;
- page-specific hero test;
- no generic photo collage;
- no invented company address, client, project, partner or certification claim;
- no fake downloadable file;
- footer and final CTA visual inspection.
