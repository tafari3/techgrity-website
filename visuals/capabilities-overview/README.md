## Purpose

This master visual proves how the complete Techgrity capability portfolio is presented as one coherent operating model rather than a long list of unrelated services.

It is the implementation authority candidate for:

- the `/capabilities` page;
- the three-part operating model;
- digital and infrastructure capability matrices;
- representative integrated-solution patterns;
- the requirement-to-handover delivery bridge;
- cross-page related-capability navigation.

## Governing content

The visual follows the locked content specification:

- H1: `Digital systems and infrastructure designed to work together`;
- hero statement connecting software, engineering, technology supply and governed delivery;
- primary CTA: `Discuss a Project`;
- secondary CTA: `How We Deliver`;
- three-part operating model;
- five Digital Systems capabilities;
- six Infrastructure capabilities;
- Technology Supply & Integration as the connecting delivery capability;
- representative solution patterns clearly distinguished from case studies;
- Discover-to-Support delivery summary;
- six industry routes;
- final integrated-project CTA.

## Visual direction

The page extends the approved homepage through:

- the same utility bar, logo header, navigation proportions and CTA treatment;
- a simpler internal-page hero with live page copy on the left and an integrated technical visual on the right;
- navy corporate authority balanced by white, ivory and pale neutral sections;
- teal used for interaction, integration and active paths;
- light, workflow-oriented treatment for Digital Systems;
- deeper navy and technical photography for Infrastructure;
- structured cards and diagrams rather than an icon wall;
- restrained shadows, radii and image grading.

## Responsive decisions

### Desktop

- complete utility bar and navigation remain visible;
- hero uses a two-column copy-and-visual composition;
- three operating-model cards appear in one row;
- Digital Systems uses one feature card plus four supporting cards;
- Infrastructure uses a six-card technical grid;
- delivery lifecycle appears as one horizontal sequence.

### Tablet

- header moves to the mobile navigation control;
- hero becomes a stacked composition;
- operating-model cards become one column;
- capability matrices reduce to two columns where readable;
- infrastructure cards use two columns;
- integration and industry layouts stack.

### Mobile

- utility bar is omitted, matching the approved homepage mobile treatment;
- the full horizontal logo and menu control remain visible;
- hero copy appears before the integrated visual;
- CTAs become full width;
- all cards become one column;
- lifecycle steps become a two-column sequence;
- matrices and lists avoid horizontal overflow.
